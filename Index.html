FAQ

Common Questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. With modern local anaesthesia and advanced techniques, a root canal feels similar to a filling. It relieves the pain caused by the infected tooth.

How many sittings does a root canal need?

Most cases are completed in a single sitting. Complex or severely infected teeth may need more than one visit; the doctor will advise after examination and an X-ray.

What is laughing gas sedation?

Laughing gas (nitrous oxide) is a safe gas mixed with oxygen, breathed through a small nose mask. You stay awake but feel calm and relaxed, and it wears off within minutes. Ideal for children and anxious adults.

Are dental implants permanent?

Implants fuse with the jawbone and can last many years with good oral hygiene and regular check-ups.
